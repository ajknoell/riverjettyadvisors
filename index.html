How We Work

For Private Equity Firms & Portfolio Companies

We build disciplined, repeatable acquisition pipelines that prioritize fit over volume, so every conversation your deal team has is worth having.

That means thorough screening before the first call, not after. We identify qualified companies, develop relationships with intent, and deliver opportunities with real context behind them.

  • Target universe development driven by your acquisition thesis
  • Researched, personalized outreach to owners and operators
  • Advisor and intermediary relationship management
  • Pipeline tracking, reporting, and conversion analysis
  • Qualified opportunity introductions with full context and rationale

Engagements are structured on a project or retainer basis. We integrate with internal deal teams or operate independently as an outsourced origination function.

For Founders & Business Owners

If you are considering a transaction or simply want to understand what your options look like, we can introduce you to private equity firms that are a genuine fit for your business.

We take the time to understand what you have built and what matters to you in a partner. That means the conversations we facilitate are with investors who share your values, understand your industry, and are aligned with your vision for the future of the business.

  • Confidential introductions to private equity firms aligned with your goals
  • Thoughtful matching based on industry focus, deal size, and partnership style
  • A relationship built on trust, not pressure

Approach

Preparation drives results. Every step is designed to ensure the right people are in the room before the conversation starts.

  1. 01

    Understand the goals on both sides

  2. 02

  3. 03

  4. 04

  5. 05

More preparation upfront means better outcomes for everyone involved. We would rather facilitate ten conversations that go somewhere than a hundred that waste people's time.
